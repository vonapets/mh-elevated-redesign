IPL treatments

Intense pulsed light, with Olivia or Madison

Reveal brighter, more even-looking skin with IPL, a light-based treatment designed to target common concerns such as sun damage, brown spots, redness, visible capillaries, and uneven skin tone.

How it works

IPL delivers gentle pulses of broad-spectrum light into the skin, targeting pigment and vascular concerns while encouraging a more refreshed, radiant appearance.

Great for

  • Sun and age spots
  • Uneven skin tone
  • Redness and visible vessels
  • Rosacea
  • Freckles and pigmentation
  • Overall skin rejuvenation
