Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

Elevated Aesthetics LLC (“Elevated Aesthetics,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you visit our website, request a consultation, book an appointment, receive treatments at our studio, or communicate with us, including by text message.

Our studio is located at 136 Jackson Street, Oshkosh, WI 54901, inside Bella Babe on the second floor.

Information we collect

We may collect information you provide directly, including:

  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • Appointment and treatment details, including services you request or receive
  • Messages you send through our consultation form or other correspondence
  • Text messaging opt-in and opt-out preferences
  • Payment and financing information when you pay for services or apply for a payment plan (processed by our payment and financing partners)

We may also collect limited information automatically when you visit our website, such as device and browser type, pages viewed, and general usage data, through cookies or similar technologies.

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to consultation requests and other inquiries
  • Schedule, confirm, remind you of, and provide appointments and services
  • Send transactional messages you have requested or that are needed to fulfill a service, such as appointment reminders, order confirmations, and account notifications
  • Send marketing and promotional messages only if you have opted in to receive them
  • Improve our website, services, and client experience
  • Process payments and payment-plan applications
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect the safety and rights of our clients and studio

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to operate our practice, including with service providers who help us with scheduling, communications, website hosting, payment processing, and financing. Those providers are permitted to use your information only to perform services for us. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Cookies and similar technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to operate the site, remember preferences, and understand how visitors use our pages. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some site features.

Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures to protect the personal information we maintain. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights and choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you by contacting us using the details below. You can opt out of marketing text messages at any time by replying STOP, and you can reply HELP for assistance. Transactional messages related to an appointment or service you requested may still be sent as needed to provide that service, unless you ask us not to contact you for those purposes. You may also unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in those messages, or by emailing us.

Children

Our services and website are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or about how we handle your information, please contact Elevated Aesthetics LLC at mh.elevated.aesthetics@gmail.com, (920) 763-7127, or 136 Jackson Street, Oshkosh, WI 54901.
