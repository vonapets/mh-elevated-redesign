Your pre and post care guide

Friendly tips for happy skin and long-lasting results from your favorite esthetician!

Book nowContact me

Before your wax

Exfoliate the area 2–3 days before your appointment to help prevent ingrown hairs. Arrive with clean, dry skin, and avoid lotions, oils, or deodorant on the treatment area. Hair should be about 1/4 inch long (the size of a grain of rice). If you’re using retinols or acne medication, pause 5 days prior to avoid irritation.

After your wax

Avoid heat, sweating, swimming, and exfoliating the area for 48 hours. Wear loose-fitting clothing to reduce irritation, keep the area clean and hydrated, and resume gentle exfoliation after 48 hours to help prevent ingrown hairs.

Chemical Peel

Before your peel

Stop using retinols and active acne products 5 days before your appointment. Two days before, switch to a gentle skincare routine to prepare your skin.

After your peel

Wear SPF daily, avoid direct sun, and keep your skincare routine simple. Don’t pick or peel flaking skin, and avoid waxing, excessive heat, and strenuous exercise for 5–7 days. Stay hydrated to support healing.

Lash Lift and Tint

Before your lift

Arrive with clean, makeup-free lashes and avoid waterproof mascara for 24 hours before your appointment.

After your lift

Keep your lashes dry for 24 hours. Avoid mascara, steam, and rubbing your eyes, and brush your lashes daily to keep them lifted. Results typically last 6–8 weeks.

Brow Lamination

Before your lamination

Avoid retinols and active acne products on the brow area for 5 days before your appointment. Arrive with clean brows.

After your lamination

Keep your brows dry for 24 hours. Brush and style them daily to maintain their lifted, fluffy appearance. Results typically last 6–8 weeks.

Wrinkle Relaxer

Before your appointment

Arrive with a clean face and avoid alcohol, ibuprofen, aspirin, fish oil, and other blood-thinning supplements for 24 hours if approved by your healthcare provider.

After your appointment

Stay upright for 4 hours and avoid rubbing the treated area. Skip strenuous exercise, saunas, and facials for 24 hours. Full results develop in 10–14 days.

Dermal Filler

Before your appointment

Stay well hydrated and avoid alcohol, ibuprofen, aspirin, fish oil, and other blood-thinning supplements for 24 hours if approved by your healthcare provider to help minimize bruising.

After your appointment

Expect mild swelling and bruising for a few days. Avoid strenuous exercise, excessive heat, and pressure on the treated area for 24–48 hours. Final results are typically seen once swelling resolves.
