Why RF

Unlike traditional microneedling, RF microneedling delivers controlled radio frequency energy beneath the surface of the skin, creating a targeted thermal effect that encourages natural skin remodeling and renewal. The result is a treatment designed to improve both skin texture and firmness over time.

  1. Microneedling meets RF Precision microneedling is combined with radio frequency energy.
  2. A targeted thermal effect Controlled radio frequency is delivered beneath the surface of the skin.
  3. Skin remodels Treatment encourages natural remodeling and renewal, designed to improve texture and firmness over time.
