Friendly tips for happy skin and long-lasting results from your favorite esthetician.

Dermaplane

Why you'll be loving it

Gently removes peach fuzz and dead skin cells for smoother, brighter skin. Enjoy flawless makeup application and enhanced skincare absorption.

Chemical peel

Before your peel

Stop using retinols and active acne products 5 days before your appointment. Two days before, switch to a gentle skincare routine to prepare your skin.

After your peel

Wear SPF daily, avoid direct sun, and keep your skincare routine simple. Don't pick or peel flaking skin, and avoid waxing, excessive heat, and strenuous exercise for 5–7 days. Stay hydrated to support healing.
